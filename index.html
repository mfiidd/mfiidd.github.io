MFIIDD

Model fitting and inference for infectious disease dynamics

A short course on fitting mechanistic models of infectious disease to data, taught by members of the Centre for the Mathematical Modelling of Infectious Diseases at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine. It covers likelihoods and Bayes' rule, MCMC and its diagnostics, model checking, particle filters and particle MCMC, observation models and approximate Bayesian computation.

Course materials → The current course, taught in Julia with Turing.jl. Freely available for anyone to work through.
Materials are open under the MIT licence. Source at github.com/mfiidd/mfiidd.
